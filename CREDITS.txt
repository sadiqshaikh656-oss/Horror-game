  "Evil Nun jumpscare evolution", plus clown laugh/scream clips supplied by the user
  (the "Well I'm Pennywise" voice line is no longer played). These are cut from
  commercial games/films: NOT cleared. Replace with self-recorded or licensed sounds.

ABOUT THE "ORIGINAL CHARACTER REDESIGN"
--------------------------------------
The Nun and the Clown in the game still use the models listed in section 2. A real
redesign needs new, original rigged 3D models and new sounds; these cannot be generated
inside this HTML project. What has been changed visually: the nun's texture was repainted (dark oxblood habit,
yellowed stained collar, grey-blue corpse skin, black sockets, stitched/blackened mouth,
black glasses frames, a brass sigil instead of the red cross) and the clown's textures and
materials were repainted (filthy stained suit, rotted cracked face paint, blood, matted hair,
glowing yellow eyes). The meshes are still the same models, so this does NOT remove the
copyright risk. What has been changed in play: the story (Darsla Hospital, the Sister,
the ritual, the separate clown entity), the clown's behaviour (his own stalking AI, not a
copy of the nun's), and the removal of the film voice line.
